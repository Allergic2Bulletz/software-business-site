Wani

Send it my way.

Private file sharing that goes straight from your device to theirs, and never sits on someone else's servers.

Take our 3-minute survey See how it works

Wani is for sending files

Pick a file, and Wani gives you a short code of four easy words. Read it to a friend, and the file goes straight from your computer to theirs.

  • No accounts and no sign-ups
  • No upload limits or expiring links
  • End-to-end encrypted the whole way
  • Nothing stored in the cloud

Wani is for hosting Ponds

A pond is your own file sharing server, running on a computer you already have at home. Set it up once, then send invitations to your family or your team.

  • Share files anytime, even when you're offline
  • Members just install the app and accept the invite
  • Your files stay on hardware you own
  • You decide who can join and what they can do
